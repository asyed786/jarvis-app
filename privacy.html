Jarvis privacy policy

Effective 9/27/2026

Jarvis is used only by its owner, Ahmed Syed.

What it does

Jarvis uses Google APIs to read the owner's own Google Calendar events and Gmail message counts and headers, and to write rows to the owner's own Google Sheets, only at his request.

Where data lives

Data is processed and stored only on the owner's computer.

What is never done with the data

Data is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or to train AI models.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions

Questions go through the developer contact listed on the app's consent screen.
